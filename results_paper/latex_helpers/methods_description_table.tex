% Brief description of the discretisation methods compared in the
% one-dimensional experiments (see src/eig_*.m).  The method labels are the
% ones used in the DOF-sweep figures and the eigenvalue comparison tables.
%
% The label differs from the one of the companion table of the plane project,
% since both snippets may be input into the same document.
%
% Requires in the preamble:
%   \usepackage{booktabs}
%   \usepackage{listings}

\begin{table}
  \centering
  \begin{tabular}{l p{0.72\linewidth}}
    \toprule
    Discretisation method & Description \\
    \midrule
    \texttt{DST} & Discrete sine transform method as described in this work. The second derivative is diagonal in the sine basis on the uniform interior grid of $N$ points, hence $-y''$ is evaluated as an inverse DST of the multiplication by $(k\pi/L)^{2}$ of the DST of $y$, $L$ being the length of the interval; the potential enters as a diagonal matrix, own implementation. \\
    \texttt{FD} & Finite difference method. Standard second-order three-point stencil on the uniform interior grid of $N$ points, the potential evaluated at the grid points, own implementation. \\
    \texttt{CDM} & Chebyshev differentiation matrix method. Second-derivative differentiation matrix at $N+2$ Chebyshev points of the second kind as implemented in the \texttt{chebfun} library, see~\cite{driscoll.ta.hale.n.ea:chebfun}, \lstinline{diffmat} function; the two boundary rows and columns are removed to impose the homogeneous Dirichlet conditions. \\
    \texttt{MBCDM} & Mapped barycentric Chebyshev differentiation matrix method. As \texttt{CDM}, with the grid pushed towards a uniform distribution by the Kosloff--Tal-Ezer map $g(s) = \arcsin(\alpha s)/\arcsin \alpha$, $\alpha \in (0, 1]$, which relaxes the $\mathrm{O}(N^{-2})$ clustering of the Chebyshev points at the endpoints; the differentiation matrix is transformed by the chain rule, own implementation on top of \texttt{chebfun}. The value $\alpha = 0.999$ is used throughout. \\
    \texttt{Numerov} & Numerov method. Fourth-order compact three-point scheme on the uniform interior grid of $N$ points, giving the generalised eigenvalue problem $\mathsf{A} y = \lambda \mathsf{B} y$ with tridiagonal $\mathsf{A}$ and $\mathsf{B}$, own implementation. \\
    \texttt{Numerov+AC} & Numerov method with the asymptotic correction. The $k$-th \texttt{Numerov} eigenvalue is shifted by the error the scheme commits on the same problem with $q = 0$, in the manner of Paine, de~Hoog and Anderssen, see~\cite{paine.jw.hoog.fr.ea:on}, which removes the leading discretisation error of the higher modes. \\
    \texttt{LGCC} & Legendre--Galerkin method with Chebyshev-computed coefficients. Galerkin discretisation on $[-1, 1]$ in the compact basis $\varphi_k = P_k - P_{k+2}$, which satisfies the homogeneous Dirichlet conditions, see~\cite{shen.j.tang.t.ea:spectral}; the stiffness matrix is diagonal and the mass matrix pentadiagonal, while the potential matrix is assembled from the Legendre coefficients of $q \varphi_j$ computed with the \texttt{chebfun} library, see~\cite{driscoll.ta.hale.n.ea:chebfun}, \lstinline{legcoeffs} function. \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
  \caption{Discretisation of the Sturm--Liouville operator $-y'' + q(x) y$ with zero Dirichlet boundary conditions.}
  \label{tab:methods_description_line}
\end{table}
